# Aura

Aura shows how much a member has given to the community. It is shown on profiles.

## How aura is earned

| Action | Aura |
| --- | --- |
| Someone sparks your post | +1 (removed if they take the spark back) |
| Someone likes your comment | +1 (removed if they unlike) |
| Your answer is marked as the solution | +15 |
| Someone joins Orbit with your invite link | +25 for you, +10 for them |
| You spark someone else's post | +1, once per post, at most 10 a day |

Sparking your own posts earns nothing, and accounts younger than 24 hours don't earn aura for sparking.

## Tiers

| Tier | Aura |
| --- | --- |
| Dust | 0 |
| Comet | 25 |
| Pulsar | 100 |
| Nebula | 300 |
| Star | 1,000 |
| Supernova | 3,000 |

Members in the top half also see a "Top X%" rank.

## What aura is used for

Aura does **not** make your posts rank higher. Its only role in ranking is the opposite: [Rising](ranking.md#rising) shows posts only from members at or below the median aura, so newer voices get a chance.

## Generous

Members who give at least twice as many sparks as they receive (and at least 50 in total) get a Generous marker on their profile.
