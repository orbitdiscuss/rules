# Moderation

How Orbit keeps the community safe, and what happens when something goes wrong.

## Automatic checks

Some checks run automatically:

- **Usernames** are checked against a list of offensive words in several languages, including common letter swaps (like `3` for `e`).
- **Posting limits** stop accounts from posting or commenting too quickly.
- **Posts and comments** are checked when they are created or edited. The check looks for: sexual content, sexual content involving minors, hate, harassment, threats and violence, self-harm, dangerous content, and spam.

What the content check can do:

| Result | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Allowed | The post or comment is published. |
| Borderline | It is published, and a person on our team reviews it. |
| Clear violation | It is not published, and your text stays in your draft so nothing is lost. |

When the check is unavailable, content is published normally rather than blocked. Direct messages are not scanned by the content check.

Every automatic block is logged, and our team can review and restore content that was blocked by mistake.

## Reports

Anyone can report a post, comment or member. You choose a reason:

`spam` · `harassment` · `hate` · `sexual` · `violence` · `self-harm` · `illegal` · `copyright` · `other`

You can add up to 500 characters of detail. Copyright reports go to a separate copyright queue (see below).

**Reports don't hide content automatically.** There is no "enough reports and it disappears" rule, because that can be abused to silence people. Every report is reviewed by a person.

## Decisions

A reviewer chooses one of:

| Decision | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| No violation | The report is closed. Nothing is removed. |
| Remove | The post or comment is removed. |
| Suspend | The content is removed and the author's account is suspended. |

All open reports about the same item are closed together. The people who reported it are told the outcome. If your content is removed, you are told which rule applied and whether automation was involved.

Other possible measures, depending on the case, are limiting features (for example messaging or video upload) or closing an account.

## Appeals

You can appeal any decision about your content or account, and any decision about a report you made:

- **Free**, within **6 months** of the decision.
- Reply to our notice, or email **support@orbitdiscuss.com** with the subject **"Appeal"**.
- A person reviews it, and we reverse the decision if it was wrong.

You can also turn to a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body or to the courts (EU Digital Services Act, Art. 20–21).

## Blocking

Blocking a member also removes any follow between the two of you, in both directions. You can unblock at any time from their profile.

## Copyright

Report copyright infringement with **Report → Copyright infringement**, or email **copyright@orbitdiscuss.com**. Valid notices lead to removal and a copyright strike for the member. Members can send a counter-notice; the content is restored unless the claimant goes to court within 10 to 14 business days.

- Strikes expire after **12 months**.
- **3 active strikes** disable video uploads.
- Serious or repeated infringement can lead to suspension or account closure.

Full details: [copyright policy](https://orbitdiscuss.com/copyright).
