# Contributing

Thanks for helping make Orbit fairer and clearer.

## Ways to help

- **Ask a question** when something here is unclear. If you wondered, others did too.
- **Propose a rule or ranking change.** Open an issue with the problem you see, a concrete proposal, and who it helps or could hurt.
- **Fix wording.** Typos, clearer sentences and better examples are welcome as pull requests.
- **Report a mismatch.** If Orbit behaves differently from what is written here, open an issue. We treat it as a bug.

## How changes are decided

Proposals are discussed in the open. The Orbit team makes the final decision, guided by safety, fairness and the law. Accepted changes are made in the product first, then documented here and listed in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## Please don't

- Report individual posts or members here. Use **Report** in the app so the right people see it quickly and privately.
- Post personal information about anyone.
- Report security vulnerabilities publicly. Email support@orbitdiscuss.com instead.

Discussions here follow the [community guidelines](guidelines.md).
