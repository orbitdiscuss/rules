# Community guidelines

Orbit is for real conversations between people from everywhere. These guidelines apply to posts, comments, direct messages, usernames, profiles and orbits. They explain the community rules in the [Terms of Service](https://orbitdiscuss.com/terms.html#rules) in plain language.

## The short version

Be someone people are glad they met. Disagree with ideas, not with people. If you wouldn't say it to someone's face, don't post it.

## Not allowed

**Harassment and bullying.** Don't target, insult, threaten or pile on someone. Don't share someone's private information. Don't keep contacting someone who has made clear they don't want it.

**Hate.** Don't attack people for who they are: ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or similar characteristics.

**Threats and violence.** Don't threaten anyone, encourage violence, or glorify attacks.

**Sexual content involving minors.** Never. We remove it, close the account and report it to the authorities. See our [child safety standards](https://orbitdiscuss.com/child-safety.html).

**Sexual content.** Keep sexual content out of Orbit.

**Self-harm.** Don't encourage or give instructions for self-harm or suicide. Talking about your own struggles and supporting others is welcome. If you or someone you know is in danger, contact local emergency services or find a helpline at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).

**Illegal and dangerous content.** Don't post content that is illegal, or instructions that could seriously hurt people.

**Spam.** No unsolicited advertising, repetitive posting, or automated mass posting or scraping without our permission.

**Impersonation and deception.** Don't pretend to be someone else, and don't mislead others about who you are.

**Copyright.** Only post content you have the right to share. See the [copyright policy](https://orbitdiscuss.com/copyright).

## Usernames

Usernames are 3 to 20 characters: letters, numbers, `_` and `-`, starting with a letter or number. Offensive names, names that imitate Orbit or staff, and look-alikes of reserved names are not accepted.

## Direct messages

Direct messages are for genuine conversations. The rules above apply in DMs too, including no unsolicited advertising. Messages are private but not end-to-end encrypted; see the [Privacy Policy](https://orbitdiscuss.com/privacy.html).

## Orbit rules

People who create an orbit can add their own rules for it. These rules are shown on the orbit page. They can make an orbit stricter or more focused, but they can't allow anything these guidelines forbid.

## Age

You must be at least 16 to use Orbit.

## If you see something

Use **Report** on any post, comment, message or profile, and **Block** to stop seeing a member. See [moderation.md](moderation.md) for what happens next.
